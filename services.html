Services

Please note: We do not currently charge for key collection / drop off. If a spare set of keys are available for us to hold onto securely for repeat bookings, that would be most appreciated.

Every cat is different, so we offer a few options depending on your needs

Drop-in Visits

  • £12 per visit
  • Up to 20 minutes
  • Perfect for cats who require little attention and single cat households.

    30 Minute Visits

  • £15 per visit
  • 30 minutes
  • Ideal for multi-cat households and those who require that little extra attention.

    One Hour Visits

  • £20 per visit
  • 60 minutes
  • If your cat is anxious when you are away or simply loves a cuddle, an hour long visit is an option.

    Day Visits

  • £40 per visit
  • Approx 10am - 2pm
  • If your cat is lively during the day and would benefit from a few hours of company, cuddles and playtime - this option may be suitable

    Overnights

  • £45 per visit
  • Approx 12 hours
  • If your cat is most active in the evening, this is the perfect option. Overnight visits include lots of playtime, as many cuddles as tolerated and morning and evening feeds.

Please note: Boundary charges may be charged depending on distance/time.

All prices will be agreed upon at time of enquiry.
